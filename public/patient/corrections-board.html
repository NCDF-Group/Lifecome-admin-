PAID SERVICE · ADULTS 18+

Sexual Health Check

Private support. Clear answers. A plan for what comes next.

Your health, handled with care

Choose a discreet contact method and discuss the tests that are right for you.

  • Clinical suitability and exposure-history review
  • Chlamydia, gonorrhoea, HIV and syphilis tests considered according to your needs
  • Urine, swab or blood samples as appropriate

A paid health check

The full fee and included tests will be shown before booking. Further tests or treatment may carry separate fees, explained in advance.

Testing too soon after an exposure may require a repeat test. Timing is confirmed by your clinician.

Urgent symptoms

Recent sexual exposure or symptoms

Contact a sexual health clinic promptly for assessment. Preventive treatment can be time-sensitive; do not wait for a routine test appointment.
