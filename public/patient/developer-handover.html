DESIGN CORRECTION · 8 NOVEMBER 2026

LifeCome Health
Services, tests and payment

Interactive demo · Corrected screen board · All screens · Route inventory

Approved structure

Four service pillars: GP Services, Professional Care, Wellbeing, and Tests and Health Checks. Insurance moves to Profile → Payment and Cover. Membership and employer benefits remain distinct funding arrangements.

Correction scope

Retired eight insurance product-sales and enquiry screens. Retained existing policy verification, service-cover decisions, documents and claims support under Payment and Cover. Added clinical testing journeys, paid Sexual Health Check, reimbursement and sample receipt layouts. Updated welcome, home, profile, messages, membership wording and service-cover options. The older public URL is retained for continuity.

Clinical testing journey

Category → suitability and safety → discreet contact preference → sample appointment → payment route → review → demo confirmation → preparation → sample tracking → clinician-reviewed results → follow-up. Under-18 and urgent concerns branch away from routine testing. Delay, repeat sample, follow-up results and cancellation have separate screens.

Pricing and funding rules

No test tariff is approved or invented. All testing is proposed, and the Sexual Health Check is a separately paid service. A live quote must state the assessment, agreed tests, collection and review fees; treatment and additional tests must be separately explained. Membership, employer and insurance checks remain pending until confirmed. Policy linking alone must never confer coverage. Pay-and-claim is not a promise of reimbursement.

Implementation requirements before launch

  • Confirm clinical governance, authorised service scope, clinic readiness, laboratory arrangements, sample handling and approved tests.
  • Implement server-side patient identity, access control, consent, audit history and discreet result notifications. This demo only uses local fictional data.
  • Clinical assessment must select sample sites, timing, repeat testing and symptom escalation; the simple demo questions do not constitute triage.
  • Integrate booking inventory, payment processor, insurer or benefit decisions, laboratory status and clinician result sign-off.
  • Confirm prices, result turnaround, cancellation and refund rules, repeat-sample policy and follow-up ownership. Never publish invented prices or guaranteed instant results.
  • Connect test appointments to shared Visits, results to Health records, and enquiries to the clinical team. Avoid putting test details into generic SMS or email notifications.

Developer route migration

The original insurance hub key is retained as Payment and Cover. Eight retired insurance-sales keys redirect to the funding hub when opened through their legacy standalone screens. Display screen numbers are regenerated; integrate using route keys rather than numeric IDs. Existing GP, Care, Wellbeing, shop and menopause routes remain.

Verification

Programmatic render, destination and state checks cover every screen, plus new age, urgent-support, test booking, funding, cancellation, unique-route and insurance-sales-removal cases. Browser review covers the new layouts and core journeys. This is a design prototype, not a live clinical or payment system.
