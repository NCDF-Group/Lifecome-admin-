Privacy notice

Help & legal

LifeComeHealthUK

Privacy notice

How this demonstration handles information

Use sample information

Do not enter personal health, identity, payment or policy information. All starting content is fictional.

Demo interaction state

Form responses and selections stay in this browser tab’s session storage. They are not submitted to a healthcare provider. Closing the tab or selecting Reset clears the demonstration state.

No connected services

Authentication, payments, messages, clinical records and insurance actions are simulated. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary access information under its own terms.

Production privacy notice

The live app must provide the approved controller identity, purposes, legal bases, retention information, recipients, user rights and contact details before personal data is collected.
